What it is

A visual personal operating system.

Ngoc OS is designed for one owner to review daily tasks, quick notes, personal activity logs and simple financial records in one place. The product is currently a private prototype and is not offered as a public social network or marketplace.

Pinterest integration

Personalized visuals, with permission.

When the Pinterest connection is enabled, Ngoc OS will use Pinterest OAuth and request read-only access needed to display or select visual inspiration from Boards and Pins that the authenticated owner has authorized.

boards:readpins:readuser_accounts:read

What it will not do

  • No automated scraping of Pinterest.
  • No publishing or editing Pins.
  • No ad campaign management.
  • No sale of Pinterest or personal data.
  • No access to content beyond permissions granted by the authenticated owner.
